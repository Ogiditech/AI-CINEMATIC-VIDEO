# 🎬 AI Cinematic Short Film: Master John KOs Poison Needle! Saves Heiress Susan!

> **"Master John KOs Poison Needle! Saves Heiress Susan! Master Sam Shocked!"**  
> A high-retention vertical 9:16 cinematic AI short film experience, complete with an interactive web simulator, 4K photorealistic keyframes, bilingual narration scripts, synthesized Web Audio sound design, and AI video generation prompts (Kling 1.5, Runway Gen-3 Alpha Turbo, Luma Dream Machine, Midjourney v6.1).

---

## 🌟 Story Synopsis

* **Act 1 — The Setup (00:00 - 00:06):** Billionaire Heiress Susan walks through a grand hotel lobby flanked by elite bodyguards, unaware that rival tycoon **Master Sam** has dispatched a lethal assassin.
* **Act 2 — The Attack (00:06 - 00:13):** The assassin strikes with a silver poison needle. Inches from Susan's neck, **Master John**—disguised as a humble worker—intercepts the wrist with iron strength.
* **Act 3 — The Knockout (00:13 - 00:21):** The assassin attacks, but Master John reveals legendary martial arts mastery, landing a thunderous knockout counter-punch!
* **Act 4 — Saving the Heiress (00:21 - 00:28):** Susan stumbles from shock and is caught in John's arms. Their eyes lock in an electrifying moment of romance and gratitude.
* **Act 5 — The Revelation (00:28 - 00:36):** Bodyguards pin down the hitman, identifying Master Sam's insignia. Susan asks John who he truly is; John replies with quiet humility.
* **Act 6 — The Shocking Offer (00:36 - 00:44):** Susan offers John an exclusive Black VIP Card and a $10M contract to become her Chief Personal Protector against Master Sam.
* **Act 7 — Cliffhanger (00:44 - 00:48):** Susan extends her hand: *"Do we have a deal, Master John?"* John stares in disbelief as his destiny turns upside down!

## 🚀 Interactive Web Simulator

The repository includes a standalone local web application providing:
- **Vertical 9:16 Cinema Player** with dynamic Ken Burns camera animations.
- **Web Audio API Engine** with synthesized real-time sound effects (tension drone, heartbeat accelerando, needle glint, 808 KO punch sub-drop, romantic string swell, and cliffhanger sting).
- **Synchronized Subtitles** in English and Chinese.
- **Live Text-to-Speech Narration** using Web Speech API.
- **Director Mode HUD** showing camera lens info, focal length, framing, and REC status.
- **One-Click Prompt Exporter** for Kling 1.5, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, and Midjourney.

### Running Locally
```bash
# Start the local server
node server.js

# Open in browser:
http://127.0.0.1:4173/
```
